Imran Khan (Imran Ahmad Khan Niazi) is a Pakistani politician and former cricketer who served as the Prime Minister of Pakistan from 2018 to 2022. Before entering politics, he was an international cricket star who led Pakistan to a World Cup victory in 1992. He is the founder of the Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf (PTI) political party. In recent years, he has been a central and divisive figure in Pakistani politics, facing various legal challenges and imprisonment while remaining a highly popular political figure among his supporters.
